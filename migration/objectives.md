# Research Objectives

★ To analyse how inflation and unemployment in Nigeria act as push factors driving migration. 

★ To evaluate the extent of occupational mismatch (“brain waste”) among Nigerian healthcare professionals in the UK. 

★ To assess the relationship between Nigerian migration to the UK and remittance inflows to Nigeria as an indicator of economic impact.
